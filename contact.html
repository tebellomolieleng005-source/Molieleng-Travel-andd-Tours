Practical help

Before you set off.

Conditions vary by route and season. Confirm details for your own trip before making travel arrangements.

When is the best time to visit?

It depends on the experience. Warmer months can suit many walks, while winter can bring snow, ice and severe conditions in high areas. Ask about the specific route and current forecast.

What should I pack?

Bring layered clothing, a waterproof outer layer, sturdy footwear, water and sun protection. A guide may recommend extra equipment for demanding routes.

Are mountain trips safe in every weather?

No route is safe in every condition. Trips may need to change, shorten or be postponed because of snow, storms, road conditions or poor visibility. Ask about the safety plan and turnaround rules.

Do I need a visa?

Entry requirements depend on your nationality, passport and travel plans. Check current official immigration guidance before travelling.

How do payment and cancellations work?

Request written terms before paying, including the deposit, balance due date, cancellation and refund policy, and what happens if weather or road conditions make a trip impossible.
